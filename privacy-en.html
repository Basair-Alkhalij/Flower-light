Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Basair Gulf – Flower Light Products Warehouse handles personal data when you use the product catalog or contact us.

Loading business information…

1. Data we may collect

When you request access to products, we may ask for your name, mobile number, and company name if you choose to provide it. We may also record limited operational data needed to secure the service and prevent misuse, as well as general catalog usage events through Google Analytics 4 when a valid measurement ID is configured.

2. Purposes of processing

We use this information to provide the catalog, communicate about products, orders and enquiries, improve the website experience, protect customer forms from automated messages and misuse, and maintain service security.

3. Protection against automated misuse

We may use Cloudflare Turnstile to verify legitimate submissions of the customer form. The verification token is checked on the server. To enforce submission limits, a salted and encrypted identifier is created from the network address; the raw IP address is not stored in the rate-limit table. Old rate-limit records are automatically removed after a short period not exceeding seven days.

4. Storage and technical service providers

Site data is stored in Supabase with access controls and permissions. Customer records that exceed the retention period configured in the business settings are deleted automatically; the default is 180 days unless changed by the owner. The site may communicate with technical services such as Cloudflare Turnstile and Google Fonts, and with Google Analytics 4 solely to measure visits and general usage when a valid measurement ID is configured. We do not use Google Analytics 4 when a measurement ID has not been configured. If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, you will be transferred to WhatsApp under its own policies. We do not sell customer data.

5. Data retention

Customer data is retained for as long as needed for the stated purposes, in accordance with the business's selected retention period, shown in the business information above when displayed. Management may delete data earlier when no longer needed or in response to a valid request in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

6. Data sharing

We share data only as necessary to operate technical services, with persons granted appropriate administrative access by the business, or when required by an applicable legal obligation.

7. Your rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or destruction of your data when applicable, or to ask how it is processed. We may request appropriate information to verify the requester's identity before fulfilling a request.

8. Browser storage and local data

The site may use local storage to remember completion of the product access form and for operational settings such as installing the web app. This information is stored on your device and can be deleted in browser settings.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when services or processing methods change. The most recent update date will appear at the top of this page. For privacy questions, please use the contact information shown in the business details above.

This page describes the site's current technical configuration. The business owner should have qualified counsel review its wording and legal obligations to ensure compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and related regulations, as applicable to the business.
